Golden Rules of Risk Management

  • The 1% Rule: Never risk more than 1% - 2% of your total account balance on a single trade.
  • Risk-to-Reward Ratio (RRR): Always aim for at least a 1:2 RRR (Risk $10 to make $20).
  • Max Daily Drawdown: Stop trading for the day if you lose 3 consecutive trades or 3% of your account.
