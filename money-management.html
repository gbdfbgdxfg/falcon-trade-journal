Golden Rules of Risk Management

1

The 1-2% Rule

Never risk more than 1% to 2% of your total account balance on a single trade entry.

2

Minimum 1:2 RRR

Always aim for a Take Profit that is at least twice your Stop Loss distance.

3

Capital Preservation

Protecting your drawdown is more important than hunting for quick profits.
